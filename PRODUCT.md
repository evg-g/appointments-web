# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: the reviewer of the live demo.** A recruiter or hiring manager for QA and test-automation
roles who opens the demo from a portfolio link and spends one to five minutes in it. They are judging
the author's work, not booking care. They walk through the story the three clinic roles tell and look
for signs of quality: does it work, is it clear, is it accessible, is it tested.

**The roles inside the story** (real product users, second in priority):

- **Patient** (`patient@aurora.test`) — books, views, and cancels their own appointments.
- **Clinician** (`clinician@aurora.test`) — sees the schedule and watches the clinic's medication
  fridges on the cold-chain dashboard; acknowledges excursions.
- **Clinic / platform admin** (`admin@aurora.test`) — manages clinics, services, and clinicians, and
  reviews the audit log.

## Product Purpose

Aurora Clinic is appointment scheduling for a network of clinics plus live monitoring of each
clinic's medication and vaccine fridges. For the demo, success is a reviewer who, in a short visit,
understands what the product does, sees it work end to end, and trusts that it is built and tested
to a professional standard.

## Positioning

Booking and cold-chain monitoring in one tool, per clinic. The cold chain is the showpiece: live
fridge temperature against a safe band, excursions that are raised after a dwell time and cleared
after a recovery time, and acknowledgement by staff. A typical booking demo cannot show this.

## Operating Context

- The web app is one of three repos: `appointments-api` (FastAPI), this app, and
  `aurora-sensor-agent` (Python IoT agent on the fridge sensor). They meet only at their contracts
  (OpenAPI; telemetry schema).
- The public demo is the production build running on its MSW mock backend with an opt-in demo
  dataset (`VITE_MSW_DEMO_DATA=true`), published to GitHub Pages at `/appointments-web/`. There is no
  real backend behind it. The same app also runs against the real API (composed stack).
- Reviewers arrive from the portfolio README, often on a laptop, sometimes on a phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Features: sign-in with role-aware navigation; booking wizard (clinic → service → clinician → time
  → confirm); appointment list, detail, transitions, and cancellation; calendar; cold-chain
  dashboard (device list, live temperature chart, device health, excursion timeline and
  acknowledgement, threshold policy); admin (clinics, services, clinicians, audit log); settings;
  light, dark, and system themes.
- Domain terms: _cold chain_, _excursion_ (temperature out of band), _dwell_ (minutes out of band
  before an excursion is raised), _recovery_ (minutes back in band before it clears), _threshold
  policy_, _device_ (fridge sensor), _audit log_.
- The API client is generated from the backend's OpenAPI contract; request types are never written
  by hand.
- Every async surface has four designed states: loading, empty (with a next action), error (with
  retry), and success.
- **Tests stay the proof.** Any UI change keeps the unit, component, E2E, accessibility, visual, and
  layout tests green; a new UI rule gets a test. Visual baselines are regenerated only for an
  intended change, and the diff is reviewed.
- The audit log shows actor and entity names; it falls back to a short id when a lookup is not
  allowed or the entity is gone (a clinic admin may not read other users' records).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Aurora Clinic**.
- Voice: **plain and clinical.** Short sentences, common words, no hype. Staff are busy and some
  content is safety-related, so wording must be exact.

## Evidence on Hand

- Demo accounts (mock backend only, password `password123`): `patient@aurora.test`,
  `clinician@aurora.test`, `admin@aurora.test`.
- Demo data (`seedDemoData` in `src/mocks/db.ts`): three clinics (Aurora Downtown, Riverside,
  Northside), four clinicians, six services, five fridge sensors with one open excursion, two weeks
  of bookings in every status, and an audit trail. The extra people are records only; the three
  sign-in accounts stay the same. Tests use the smaller base seed.
- Screenshots of the real rendered pages: `docs/screenshots/` (regenerated by `npm run screenshots`).
- **Absent, and must not be invented:** real clinics or customers, testimonials, usage numbers,
  uptime or performance claims, certifications, and real sensor readings. The demo is labelled as a
  demo with no real backend.

## Product Principles

1. **Show it working in minutes.** A reviewer should reach the cold-chain chart and a completed
   booking without instructions beyond the demo sign-in hint.
2. **The cold chain leads.** It is the part only this product has; give it the first and clearest
   view.
3. **Honest by default.** Demo data is labelled as demo data; nothing claims more than the code does.
4. **Quality is visible and verified.** Accessibility, the four async states, and responsive layout
   are part of every surface, and each is backed by a test.
5. **Plain words over clever ones,** especially for anything about temperature, safety, or
   cancellations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every route passes the axe gate with zero serious or critical violations in both light
and dark themes. Keyboard operable throughout, with visible focus and correct ARIA. Respect
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
